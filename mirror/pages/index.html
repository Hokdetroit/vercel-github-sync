Your Studio Escrow Questions, Answered

What does Studio Escrow cost?

Growth is $97/month, Scale is $197/month, and Medical Suite is $597/month per practice location. Mortgage Suite combines Scale at $197/month with a separate one-time buildout starting at $3,000. Messaging, calling, AI usage, and implementation may carry additional charges. Confirm your total cost, limits, and terms before subscribing; no annual savings or unlimited usage is promised here.

Which industries is Studio Escrow built for?

Studio Escrow supports real estate, mortgage, medical and wellness, home services, agencies, e-commerce, restaurants, events, and professional services. Industry-specific setups connect your pipeline, communications, and workflows.

What can the AI tools do?

Studio Escrow advertises AI tools for reception, conversations, follow-up, content, reporting, and workflow assistance. Scale includes AI Studio and Claude integration. Confirm availability, usage allowances, and charges for your plan. Configure the tools for your business and review AI outputs; they do not replace professional judgment.

What is included in the Medical Suite?

Medical Suite includes a HIPAA-ready CRM, BAA, encrypted patient communications, AI receptionist, secure scheduling, review generation, and referral tracking. It complements your EHR. Each practice remains responsible for its own comprehensive HIPAA compliance program.

How do I get started?

Book a demo to choose your plan and setup. Then connect your brand, import contacts, configure pipelines, and activate your workflows. Migration and implementation needs vary by business; do not cancel existing tools until your new setup is tested.

What is the Mortgage Suite?

The Mortgage Suite is a done-for-you CRM and marketing setup for loan officers, built by House of Karma. It can include branding, a website, CRM setup, lead connections, trained AI agents, and mortgage nurture workflows. Scale is $197/month; buildouts start at $3,000.
