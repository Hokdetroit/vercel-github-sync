Health CPG Founders Ask Us These Every Week

Can you run ads for health supplement and CPG brands?

Yes. We've scaled health CPG brands from launch to 7 figures across supplements, functional beverages, topicals, nootropics, and wellness products. Platform policies exist for health claims - but we know how to build creative that converts and clears on first review.

What's a realistic CAC for a health CPG brand?

It depends on your product, AOV, and funnel. We've driven 93% CAC reductions for some brands and 186%+ purchase uplifts for others. But the number that actually matters is CAC:LTV - and we help you lock it down and optimize it from day one.

Do you work with Shopify brands?

Yes - the majority of our health CPG clients are on Shopify. We're deeply familiar with the ecosystem including subscription apps, conversion tracking, bundle mechanics, and ecomm funnel optimization.

How do you approach subscription vs one-time purchase?

Both. We build acquisition funnels optimized for your primary offer and email/SMS flows designed to convert one-time buyers into subscribers. The goal is always to grow LTV - not just close the first sale.

How long until we see results?

Most brands see results within the first 30 days. First creative wins typically land in 2-4 weeks and full system performance compounds from there. Health CPG rewards founders who ship quickly and iterate - and that's how we operate.

How do I know you're actually incentivized to perform?

Every engagement we run has a performance component built in - we're structured to be aligned with your growth, not just your retainer. For brands at $1M+ MRR, we go further with Partner-tier deals that include performance bonuses tied to specific growth milestones. Either way, we move fast because we have skin in the game.

What makes you different from other agencies?

We've scaled 150+ health and wellness brands and we have the case studies to prove it. We know the work is good because other agencies have copied our ad frameworks and strategies after we've proven them. We take that as a compliment. While others are still figuring out health CPG, we've been running these playbooks for years. What we actually care about is building real partnerships - several of our clients have been with us for years because we put in the work, stay honest, and treat your growth like it's ours.

What kind of media budgets do you work with?

We've worked with brands spending anywhere from $3K/month to over $2M/month. Whether you're scaling from zero or already operating at volume, we have the systems and team in place to manage performance.
