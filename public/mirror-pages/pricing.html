Questions, answered

Which plan should I start with?

Most businesses start on Growth for the core CRM, inbox, websites and automation. Move to Scale when you want advanced workflows and AI Studio. Healthcare practices and loan officers should look at the dedicated suites.

Are there usage charges beyond the monthly price?

Yes. Messaging, calling, AI usage and implementation may carry additional charges. Confirm your total cost, limits and allowances with Studio Escrow before subscribing.

How is the Medical Suite priced for multiple locations?

The Medical Suite is $597 per month for each practice location, and every location is covered by a BAA. It works alongside your existing EHR, and your practice remains responsible for its HIPAA compliance program.

What does the Mortgage Suite buildout include?

House of Karma builds your branding, website, CRM setup and lead-source connections on the Scale plan. Buildouts start at $3,000 as a one-time cost, alongside Scale at $197 per month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move between plans as your business grows. Talk to the Studio Escrow team to plan the switch so your workflows carry over cleanly.
