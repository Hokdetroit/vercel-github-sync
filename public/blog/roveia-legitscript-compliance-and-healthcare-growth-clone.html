Roveia + LegitScript: Building Compliance Into Healthcare Growth From Day One

Roveia partners with LegitScript to bring healthcare certification into the growth process earlier, connecting compliance requirements with the website, creative, paid media, funnels, and patient acquisition infrastructure healthcare brands need to launch and scale.

Roveia Health Team
October 15, 2026

Healthcare Growth Starts Before the First Ad Goes Live

Healthcare companies operate under a different set of rules than most consumer brands.

A telehealth company can’t simply build a landing page, create a Meta campaign, and start spending. The business model, website language, licenses, prescription offerings, clinical relationships, patient disclosures, privacy practices, advertising claims, and payment infrastructure can all affect how the company is able to market itself.

For many telemedicine providers and other regulated healthcare businesses, LegitScript Healthcare Certification is also part of the path to advertising and payment processing.

LegitScript certification is recognized by major advertising and payment platforms and helps businesses demonstrate that they meet LegitScript’s standards for operating in regulated healthcare categories. Depending on the business model and market, certification may be required as part of gaining access to advertising opportunities on platforms such as Google, Meta, Microsoft, TikTok, and LinkedIn, as well as working with major payment networks.

That creates an important operational question:

Why wait until certification is complete to start building everything required to grow?

Roveia and LegitScript work together so certification and marketing preparation can happen as connected parts of the same launch strategy.

‍

What LegitScript Certification Actually Does

LegitScript Healthcare Certification is designed for businesses operating in regulated healthcare categories, including telemedicine providers, online pharmacies, medical spas, digital health platforms, and other healthcare merchants.

The certification process evaluates areas including business and professional licensing, legal compliance, patient services, business relationships, prescription practices, privacy, advertising, and transparency.

For healthcare businesses, certification can serve several practical purposes.

It can demonstrate to platforms and business partners that the company has undergone an independent compliance review. It can also help satisfy requirements associated with advertising and card-not-present payment processing in regulated healthcare categories.

LegitScript currently monitors healthcare and other high-risk industries across more than 100 countries and has certified more than 10,000 websites.

Certification is important, but it isn’t the entire go-to-market process.

A certified company still needs an effective website, clear positioning, compliant creative, working tracking, high-converting landing pages, lifecycle marketing, and an acquisition strategy capable of turning advertising access into actual patients.

That’s where the Roveia partnership comes in.

‍

How Roveia and LegitScript Work Together

LegitScript provides the certification framework. Roveia brings that framework into the growth process.

Rather than treating certification as a standalone task that happens before marketing begins, Roveia incorporates the requirements affecting the brand’s go-to-market strategy while building the marketing infrastructure.

That can mean reviewing how the business model is represented on the website, making sure landing pages and advertising plans reflect the realities of the healthcare category, preparing creative, setting up tracking, developing paid media campaigns, and building email and SMS flows while certification is moving forward.

The objective isn’t to bypass or shorten LegitScript’s review - it’s to make sure the work happening around certification supports what the business needs to do once it’s ready to market.

When the two workstreams are disconnected, companies can reach the end of certification only to discover that their website needs significant revisions, their advertising language creates problems, their landing pages aren’t ready, or their acquisition infrastructure hasn’t been built.

Starting both earlier can reduce that unnecessary rework.

‍

Why Certification Should Be Part of the Growth Strategy From the Beginning

LegitScript’s own certification checklist shows how broad the compliance review can be.

Healthcare applicants may need to provide business registrations, professional licenses, pharmacy or manufacturer licenses where applicable, clinician information, telemedicine documentation, privacy policies, and information about business partners and operations.

Those aren’t details that exist separately from marketing.

They influence what the website says, which services can be promoted, how the patient experience is explained, which partners appear in the funnel, and what claims the brand can responsibly make.

For a telehealth company entering a new category, waiting until the website is finished to think about certification can create avoidable problems.

A better approach is to build both together.

Certification informs the operating requirements. Marketing turns those requirements into a patient experience that is clear, credible, and capable of converting.

Step 1: Start With the Business Model, Not the Ads

Before building campaigns, the company needs clarity around what it actually does.

Is it providing telemedicine directly?

Is it facilitating care through affiliated clinical groups?

Which pharmacies, providers, manufacturers, or fulfillment partners are involved?

Where does the company operate?

What medications or healthcare services are being offered?

Those answers influence certification, platform eligibility, website requirements, and advertising strategy. Roveia can use that information to build a marketing plan around the actual healthcare operation instead of designing a funnel first and trying to make compliance fit afterward.

Step 2: Build the Website and Certification Requirements Together

Healthcare websites have to do more than convert traffic.

They also need to accurately communicate who provides care, how treatment works, what the business offers, how patient information is handled, and what relationships exist between the different entities involved in delivering the service.

LegitScript evaluates areas such as patient services disclosure, affiliates and partners, prescription validity, privacy, advertising, and transparency as part of its certification standards.

That makes the website part of the compliance foundation.

While certification is underway, Roveia can develop or refine the conversion side of the same experience: page structure, positioning, calls to action, intake flow, landing pages, analytics, and conversion tracking.

The company isn’t building one site for certification and another for customers, it’s building one healthcare experience that needs to do both jobs well.

Step 3: Prepare Paid Media Before Certification Becomes the Bottleneck

Certification can affect eligibility to advertise in regulated healthcare categories, but certification itself doesn’t build the campaigns.

Creative still needs to be produced. Audiences need to be planned. Landing pages need to be tested. Tracking needs to work. Google Ads structures need to be built. Meta campaigns need appropriate creative and messaging.

Roveia can prepare those pieces while the certification process is moving forward.

This changes what happens after certification.

Instead of beginning the entire marketing build after receiving approval, the brand can already have much of its acquisition infrastructure prepared. That’s the difference between treating certification as the end of one project and treating it as part of a coordinated market launch.

Step 4: Understand That Certification Doesn’t Guarantee Ad Approval

LegitScript certification can make healthcare companies eligible for advertising opportunities that require third-party certification, but it doesn’t guarantee that an individual ad, landing page, account, or campaign will be approved.

Advertising platforms still apply their own policies.

Google, for example, requires advertisers promoting certain prescription drug services to complete Google’s own healthcare certification process, and in some markets LegitScript certification is one of the third-party requirements that telemedicine businesses must satisfy.

Platform rules can also restrict what prescription-drug terms appear in ads or landing pages and how certain healthcare services are promoted.

That’s why compliance and performance marketing need to stay connected after certification. The brand still needs campaigns designed around the actual policies of the platforms it intends to use.

Step 5: Launch With the Growth Infrastructure Already in Place

Once a brand is ready to market, certification should connect directly into execution.

That means paid media can launch into landing pages designed for conversion. Leads can enter functioning email and SMS sequences. Analytics can show where prospects are dropping out. Creative testing can begin with a clear understanding of the category’s advertising limitations.

Roveia’s role extends across that entire acquisition system, including paid media, creative, conversion optimization, landing pages, email, SMS, and patient acquisition strategy.

LegitScript establishes an independent certification framework for operating within regulated healthcare categories. Together, those capabilities connect compliance with the work required to actually acquire patients.

‍

Measuring the Partnership Beyond Certification

The most useful partnership metrics aren’t simply downstream advertising results - they show whether certification and marketing preparation are creating a clearer path to market.

Roveia and LegitScript can evaluate the partnership using metrics such as:

  • ~30 LegitScript-certified healthcare and telehealth clients scaled by Roveia
  • 2.5 weeks average time from certification kickoff to approval
  • 2-3 days average time from certification to first campaign live
  • 6X+ ROAS and 74% lower CAC in 3 months for one prescription telehealth platform

These numbers should remain separate from marketing performance metrics such as CAC and ROAS.

The certification metrics show how the partnership supports market readiness.The growth metrics show what Roveia can do once the foundation is in place.

‍

What Happens After the Foundation Is Built?

Compliance gets a healthcare brand into a position to market responsibly, performance marketing determines what happens next.

For one telehealth weight-loss provider, Roveia built the acquisition system from the ground up around the requirements of a highly regulated category. The work included paid media, conversion-focused landing pages, tracking infrastructure, creative, Google and Meta campaigns, and email and SMS lifecycle marketing.

The company launched with a $130 customer acquisition cost and generated more than $10,000 in email and SMS revenue in under 30 days.

Those results didn’t come from certification alone - they came from pairing the right compliance foundation with an acquisition system designed specifically for healthcare.

‍

Compliance Also Supports More Efficient Growth at Scale

Another prescription telehealth platform came to Roveia with a different challenge.

The company was already acquiring customers, but the economics needed significant improvement.

Roveia restructured paid media, strengthened retargeting, reorganized Google Ads, developed stronger creative, and concentrated spend around higher-intent prospects.

The result was 6X+ ROAS, a 74% reduction in CAC, and 2X purchase volume.

Again, these are marketing outcomes, not LegitScript-attributable results. But they demonstrate why the growth system surrounding certification matters. Getting permission to advertise is valuable.Knowing how to turn that access into profitable patient acquisition is what creates business growth.

‍

Certification and Performance Marketing Serve the Same Business Goal

Healthcare companies sometimes treat certification as legal administration and marketing as a completely separate department.

In practice, both affect the same outcome:

Can the company reach the right patients, operate responsibly, and build a sustainable business?

LegitScript focuses on verifying that healthcare businesses meet its certification standards and helping qualified merchants access important advertising and payment opportunities. Roveia focuses on converting that market access into growth through healthcare-specific acquisition strategy and execution.

The partnership connects those pieces.

Certification requirements can inform the website before it’s finished. Advertising considerations can be incorporated before creative is produced. Landing pages can be built around the realities of the healthcare category. Tracking and lifecycle marketing can be ready before acquisition begins.

Instead of fixing those pieces one at a time after launch, the company starts with a more complete foundation.

‍

Who Is This Approach Built For?

The partnership is particularly relevant for companies operating where healthcare, digital acquisition, and regulated products overlap.

That includes telemedicine providers, prescription-enabled health platforms, weight-management companies, men’s and women’s health businesses, medical spas, online healthcare providers, and other healthcare companies whose growth depends on digital advertising and payment processing.

LegitScript itself identifies telehealth advisory firms, marketing agencies, and go-to-market specialists as organizations that can incorporate certification into the services they provide clients through its Enterprise Referral Partner model.

That reflects an important change in digital healthcare. Compliance can no longer sit in one department while growth happens somewhere else.The two systems increasingly have to be designed together.

‍

Final Take

Healthcare brands shouldn’t have to choose between building for compliance and building for growth - they need both.

Roveia’s partnership with LegitScript creates a more connected approach by bringing certification requirements into the go-to-market process early, while the website, funnels, creative, tracking, paid media, and lifecycle marketing are still being built.

LegitScript helps healthcare businesses demonstrate that they meet its certification standards and access important advertising and payment opportunities.

Roveia builds the acquisition infrastructure needed to turn that access into patients and revenue.

Certification and performance marketing are two halves of the same goal: getting healthcare brands ready to market responsibly and giving them the infrastructure to grow once they do.
